Better aerodynamics, by design.

Airfoil optimization is a crucial aspect of aerodynamic design in aerospace engineering. The objective is to enhance the aerodynamic performance of an airfoil by improving its lift, drag, and other performance characteristics.

This project focuses on the optimization of the NACA 64A210 airfoil using ANSYS software as the solver. The objective is to improve the aerodynamic performance of the airfoil by optimizing key parameters such as lift, drag, and lift-to-drag ratio.

Optimized airfoils play a vital role in achieving improved fuel efficiency, maneuverability, stability, and overall performance of aircraft and other aerodynamic systems.

The overall procedure provides valuable insights into the design and performance enhancement of airfoils in the aerospace industry and other domains that require efficient, high-performance aerodynamic designs.
